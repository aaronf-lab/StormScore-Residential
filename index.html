The idea, in three stages

STAGE 01

The list builds itself

Storms leave a footprint, and that footprint is knowable the same night it happens — which streets, which neighborhoods, which side of town.

So the target list is finished before anyone puts a door hanger in a truck. Nobody drives a grid hoping. The list arrives already sorted.

STAGE 02

Your bots make the calls

You already run voice AI at volume for real estate. The infrastructure exists, the compliance work is done, the thing answers the phone and holds a conversation today.

Point it at the storm list instead. Same technology, same guardrails — a different script and a different reason to call. That is the entire lift.

STAGE 03

Warm hands, local rep, same day

Conversations that go somewhere get routed to the rep who covers that ground, with the appointment already on the calendar.

Across thirty offices, that turns the hours after a storm — the hours that decide who gets the job — into something that runs whether or not anyone is awake.

And then there's the commercial side

The same logic points at commercial buildings, where one good conversation is worth a great many residential ones. Nobody is doing this well. That is the part I find hardest to stop thinking about.

There's real work between here and there, and I'd rather build it with you than hand you a pitch deck.

So: happy birthday. The idea is yours if you want it.
